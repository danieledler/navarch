Navarch GitHub

Keep every project on course.

Navarch is a macOS planner that keeps work across many folders pointed at 3–6 month goals, while Claude Code sessions in the Claude desktop app do the work. Its phone app keeps watch while you’re away.

On your phone

Away from your Mac, the phone app shows the sessions waiting for you, the week, your goals and your plan limits. Its + button adds a task that lands in Navarch on your Mac right away.

Off until you turn it on
Switch on phone access from the Mac’s status bar, and Navarch serves the phone app from your Mac.
Inside your tailnet
Navarch points Tailscale Serve at it, so your phone reaches your Mac over your own tailnet, not the public internet. You can give it another address you run yourself instead.
Paired with a QR code
Scan it, pair with a single-use code that lasts ten minutes, and add the app to your Home Screen. Resetting the token unpairs every phone.

What it keeps in view

Spaces, projects, tasks
Group your folders into spaces, the way Slack groups workspaces. Each folder is a project with a task tree as deep as you need.
Goals for the period
Star tasks at any level as goals for the current 3–6 month period, and see how far along each one is.
The week
Pin tasks to this week. Navarch adds what’s due, what you worked on and what you finished.
Every session in its place
Each Claude Code session shows up under its project with its status, your last message, its processes and CPU. Time and tokens roll up to tasks, projects and spaces, and badges count the sessions waiting for your input or holding a reply you haven’t read.
Start from a task
Start a session from a task and it opens in the Claude app’s Code tab with the task as its prompt. The new session links to the task by itself.
Plan limits
The status bar shows your 5-hour and weekly Claude plan usage. As a limit gets close, Navarch can tell running sessions, through a Claude Code hook, to save their work and schedule a resume before they are cut off.
A phone app
Check what’s waiting for you, the week, your goals and your plan limits from your phone, and add tasks that show up on your Mac at once. It reaches your Mac over Tailscale, and only once you turn it on.

Where your data stays

Reads
The session files and transcripts the Claude desktop app keeps on your Mac. Navarch only reads them and never writes to them.
Keeps
Your spaces, projects, tasks and goals, in its own SQLite database on your Mac.
Sends
One request: your plan usage, fetched from Anthropic with the Claude Code login already in your Keychain.
Serves
Nothing, unless you turn on phone access. Then it serves the phone app to your tailnet through Tailscale Serve (or the address you give it), and your data only to phones paired with its token. Nothing else leaves your Mac.

Build it

Navarch runs on macOS and is built from source. You need the Claude desktop app, Rust, Node and pnpm. Phone access is optional and needs Tailscale on your Mac and your phone.

# get the code
git clone https://github.com/danieledler/navarch.git
cd navarch
pnpm install

# try it in a development window
pnpm tauri dev

# build Navarch.app and install it in /Applications
scripts/install-app.sh

Development builds work on a copy of Navarch’s database. Only the app installed in /Applications uses the real one.
